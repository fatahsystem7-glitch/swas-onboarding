Frequently asked questions

Do I need to change my phone or SIM? +

No. We give you a new local number that forwards to you, and the missed-call text-back works automatically — nothing to install.

How long until I'm live? +

Most trades are live within about 7 days, once regulatory checks on your local number clear.

What are the compliance documents for? +

UK local numbers require proof of ID and address (Ofcom rules). You can upload these right after signup — we handle the submission for you.

Can I cancel? +

Yes — there's no long tie-in. Cancel any time from your dashboard.
